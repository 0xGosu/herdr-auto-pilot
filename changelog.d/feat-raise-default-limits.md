- Raised the default runaway-loop limits to 50 consecutive auto-prompts and 10 auto-prompts per minute per agent (was 30 and 5); a config that sets these keys explicitly keeps its own values
- Tightened four built-in safety rules (credential rotation, revoking or rotating keys and tokens, deploying or publishing to production, and destructive verbs aimed at data) so they stop escalating on narration: past tense ("dropped the card on the table"), a verb and a target in different sentences or split by a code span, an app's own "stream token", and lines that mention a release and production without shipping to production. The rule ids change, so an old `disabled_seed_patterns` entry for one of them no longer applies.
